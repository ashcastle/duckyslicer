# DuckySlicer governance

DuckySlicer is currently maintained by [@ashcastle](https://github.com/ashcastle).
Contributors retain authorship of accepted work; merge, release, and security-response
authority remains with the maintainer until additional maintainers are named publicly.

## Decisions

- User-facing and architectural changes are discussed in a focused issue or pull request.
- The maintainer decides after considering compatibility, offline operation, mobile
  usability, maintenance cost, test evidence, licensing, and security.
- Vulnerabilities use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public
  design discussion.
- A rejected proposal may be reconsidered when constraints or evidence change.

## Changes and releases

Every merge must preserve licensing and attribution, pass the proportionate local
gate, and leave rollback or recovery practical. Release authority is not delegated to
automation: candidates follow [docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md), and publication
remains an explicit maintainer action.

Additional maintainers may be appointed after sustained, trusted contributions. Any
change to ownership or release authority is recorded in this document and CODEOWNERS
before it takes effect.
