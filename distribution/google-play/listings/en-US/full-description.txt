Prepare 3D prints directly on your Android phone or tablet, fully offline.

DuckySlicer lets you import a model, choose printer and filament profiles, slice on your device, inspect every layer, and export G-code. Your projects stay on your device unless you choose to export them or send G-code to a printer.

What you can do
• Import STL, OBJ, and 3MF models
• Move, rotate, scale, arrange, split, and cut objects
• Paint supports, seams, and filament colors
• Choose from broad printer, filament, and slicing profile libraries
• Adjust detailed quality, strength, speed, support, and other settings
• Preview the full layer range with toolpath colors, time, and filament estimates
• Export G-code
• Optionally connect directly to OctoPrint or Klipper/Moonraker

Made for mobile
• One-finger and two-finger controls
• Phone layout and a wider tablet layout
• Device-language localization with English as the default
• No account or DuckySlicer cloud service required

DuckySlicer is an open-source project under active development. Check printer compatibility and review G-code before printing.
