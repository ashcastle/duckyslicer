# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

DuckySlicer contributors are expected to make participation welcoming and safe.
Be respectful, discuss ideas and code rather than people, accept constructive
feedback, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports
should be made through a private maintainer contact when available. Do not post
personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will protect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as practical
and will apply these expectations consistently to maintainers and contributors.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, project
chat, events, and public interactions where someone is representing DuckySlicer.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
